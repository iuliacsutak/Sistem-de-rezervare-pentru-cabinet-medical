1. Instalati Node.js (https://nodejs.org/en/download)
2. Instalti MongoDB (https://www.mongodb.com/try/download/community)
3. Clonati proiectul (https://github.com/emacoconu/Sistem-de-rezervare-pentru-cabinet-medical)
4. Deschideti un terminal in folderul cu proiectul
5. Rulati comanda "npm i"
6. Rulati comanda "npm run dev"
7. Accesati pagina web pe http://localhost:3000/
